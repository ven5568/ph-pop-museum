Celebrity & Digital Culture

Fan communities grew rapidly through Friendster and text voting, making celebrities feel more accessible and interactive.

Globalization & Transnational Media

Asianovelas like Meteor Garden and anime shaped local fashion, slang, and television programming.

Youth Identity Formation

Filipino youth combined foreign trends (like low-rise jeans) with local values (like barkada culture) to create a unique 2000s identity.

Room 05 — Forms of Pop Culture

The mediums of the decade.

Popular culture in the 2000s was experienced primarily through broadcast media (TV and Radio), print (Magazines), and the rapidly emerging internet (Friendster, early blogs).

Room 06 — Patterns, Continuity & Change

The bridge to the 2010s.

The 2000s served as a transitional bridge. The patterns of youth expression and foreign influence remained, but the emergence of SMS and social networking began shifting the culture from broadcast-centered to networked.

Historical Timeline

Key moments from 2000 to 2010.

2000-2002: The peak of Y2K fashion, the rise of SMS texting, and the early days of internet cafés.

2003-2006: The OPM rock renaissance, the explosion of Friendster, and the dominance of Asianovelas on TV.

2007-2010: The rise of competitive gaming (DotA, CS), and the gradual shift toward the social media landscape of the 2010s.

About the Project

Tracing the Pop: A Decade in PH Pop Culture (2000–2010) is an interactive virtual museum. It explores how Philippine popular culture served as a transitional bridge between broadcast-centered culture and the increasingly networked culture of the 2010s.
